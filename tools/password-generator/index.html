FAQ

What length should I use?

Use at least 16 characters for most accounts and longer for shared secrets.

Are passwords stored?

No. They are generated locally and not saved.

Can I avoid symbols?

Yes. Enter a length followed by no symbols, for example: 24 no symbols.
