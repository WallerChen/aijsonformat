FAQ

What whitespace is removed?

The main action removes spaces, tabs and line breaks. Normalize Spaces keeps words readable with single spaces.

Can I remove line breaks only?

Use Text Formatter or Line Sorter for line-based cleanup.

Is the text uploaded?

No. Whitespace cleanup runs locally in your browser.
