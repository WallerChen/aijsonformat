FAQ

Is MD5 secure?

No. MD5 is not recommended for passwords or security-sensitive signatures.

What is MD5 useful for?

It is still useful for quick fingerprints, legacy checksums and simple comparisons.

Is the hash generated locally?

Yes. The text is processed in your browser.
