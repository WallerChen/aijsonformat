Contact AI JSON Format

The fastest way to reach us depends on what you need. Pick the channel that matches your topic and we will route it to the right person.

Email

For privacy requests, advertising enquiries, takedown notices, partnership ideas or anything that needs a private reply, write to hello@aijsonformat.com. We typically respond within two business days.

Bug reports and feature requests

If a tool returns the wrong output, the page renders incorrectly, or you have an idea for a new utility, the best place is the GitHub issue tracker at github.com/WallerChen/aijsonformat/issues. Public reports help other users find the same answer and get fixed faster than email.

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • The URL of the tool you were on.
  • A short description of what you expected vs what happened.
  • The shortest possible input that reproduces the issue.
  • Browser and operating system, when relevant.

Advertising and partnerships

The site is supported by display advertising. For direct ad placement, sponsorship of specific tool categories or partnerships with related developer products, email hello@aijsonformat.com with the subject line "Advertising" and a brief description of your offering.

Privacy and data requests

If you want to know what data, if any, we hold about you, or you want to exercise rights under GDPR or similar regulations, email hello@aijsonformat.com with the subject line "Privacy". See the privacy policy for the full picture before writing — most user data is not transmitted to our servers in the first place.

Press and writers

For press enquiries, citation requests or interviews, the same email address works. We are happy to share screenshots, logos and a one-paragraph project description on request.
